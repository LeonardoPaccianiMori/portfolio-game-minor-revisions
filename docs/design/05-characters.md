# 05 — Characters and Voice

Status: **Documented — approved by Leonardo on 2026-09-10 (Block A5); revised the same day to make the PI a static caricature.**

## Purpose

This block defines the three named characters, the player character, their
distinct voices, and how each reacts to the player's complicity.

## Cast (approved)

| Character                 | Role              | Voice                         | Arc                                                                                                |
| ------------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dr. Helena Voss (she/her) | Tenured PI        | Cheerful procedural euphemism | None; a static caricature. Tenured, secure, and indifferent to junior careers; no humanising scene |
| Dario Ferreira (he/him)   | PhD student       | Anxious precision             | Does the invisible work; the player can protect or exploit him; reacts immediately to complicity   |
| Mara Lindqvist (she/her)  | Departing postdoc | Deadpan survival humour       | The player's possible future; notices complicity late; solidarity or competition                   |

## Dr. Helena Voss (approved revision)

- Tenured and untouchable; she does not change and is not redeemed.
- Comic devices: selective memory of her own instructions, weaponised
  positivity, instant reframing, credit capture, blaming individuals for
  structural failure, wellness advice that shifts responsibility, and
  contradictions delivered as clarity.
- She is absurd in content and plausible in delivery. She never admits malice
  and is always "helpful"; the institution treats her as normal. This keeps the
  caricature funny and threatening instead of cartoonish.
- The fellowship gives her new material: she refuses to read drafts, gives one
  late contradictory comment that can stale a section, demands sponsor credit
  while contributing nothing, and promises a support letter that arrives late
  and generic. If the player fabricates and she is implicated, she distances
  herself instantly.

## The player character (approved)

- Chosen name and pronouns.
- A lightly authored voice: dry, observant, controlled.
- Stance-based choices without moral labels.
- The player is not a blank slate: the writing gives the protagonist opinions
  through the internal voice, while the decisions remain the player's.

## Comic mechanisms (approved)

- **Voss:** funding prose, impact statements, euphemism, selective memory, and
  weaponised positivity.
- **Dario:** anxious precision, over-clarification, and the fear of doing it
  wrong.
- **Mara:** deadpan, short sentences, and jokes used as exits.
- **The player:** internal dry commentary on the gap between what is said and
  what is meant.

## Reactions to complicity (approved)

- Dario reacts immediately: fear, anger, or withdrawal.
- Mara notices late and then decides what to do with it.
- Voss uses it, may quote it back to the player later, and distances herself if
  it becomes a risk.
- On the fellowship, Dario's data can be exploited by the player, and Mara
  warns that the proposal is a trap.

## The reviewer chorus (approved)

- Reviewers exist only inside the review set piece: absurd archetypes, not full
  characters.
- The editor's messages are institutional voice, not a person.
- No real person or institution is depicted.
