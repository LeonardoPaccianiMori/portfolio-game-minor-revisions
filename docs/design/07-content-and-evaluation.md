# 07 — Content and Evaluation

Status: **Documented — approved by Leonardo on 2026-09-10 (Block A7).**

## Purpose

This block defines how much authored content the game has, what forms it takes,
how it is stored and validated, and how we will know whether it works.

## Volume and shape (approved)

- Word budget: 8,000–12,000 unique English words, mostly dialogue and scene
  text. The fellowship adds form and panel text within this range; if content
  planning exceeds it, a small amendment is brought for approval.
- About ten fixed authored scenes across the three acts. The rest of the story
  arrives through systemic and interactive text.
- One run: 60 minutes maximum. Three or four sacrifice paths invite a second
  run.

## Content types (approved)

- In-engine scenes, with no cutscenes.
- Messages and emails.
- Notices, forms, and policy text.
- Desk-board text: claims, evidence, and requirements.
- Reviewer reports inside the review set piece and the short fellowship panel.
- Fellowship form text and Voss's feedback.
- Four endings plus the final personnel file.

## Content data (approved)

- Stable identifiers and authored data files, validated at build.
- No generated dialogue at runtime.
- The exact format and schema are defined in Phase B.

## Language (approved)

- English only.
- Plain comprehension is required. Insider recognition is rewarded, but it is
  never necessary.

## Evaluation (approved, with a caveat)

- Leonardo tests the game directly. This is the primary human evidence.
- Agent-run comprehension probes and automated checks provide supporting
  evidence.
- Limited informal playtesting with trusted people is permitted but **not
  guaranteed**. It involves no data collection, no research framing, and no
  public exposure. If no outside testers are available, evaluation proceeds on
  Leonardo's testing plus agent evidence, and the limitation is recorded
  honestly.
- No telemetry, analytics, accounts, or external reporting.

## Humour and comprehension checks (approved)

- Per-act joke-density review and a clean-context comprehension probe.
- Measured rather than assumed: we record what was tested and what was found,
  including negative results.

## Content safety (approved)

- Fictional, non-actionable science: no real protocol, treatment claim, or
  unsupported real capability.
- Level 2 darkness boundary: permanent consequences and institutional dread are
  allowed; graphic self-harm, gore, and explicit abuse are not.
- A content note appears at the start.
