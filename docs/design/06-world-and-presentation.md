# 06 — World and Presentation

Status: **Documented — approved by Leonardo on 2026-09-10 (Block A6).**

## Purpose

This block defines the setting, the floor, movement and camera, the visual
language, audio direction, the desk board, the asset approach, the
accessibility baseline, and environmental comedy.

## Setting (approved)

Ashgrove University, Department of Terrestrial Ecology. Fictional; rename
freely.

| Space       | Function                          | Presentation notes                              |
| ----------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Desk hub    | Analysis, writing, the desk board | Shared office, personal clutter, the board      |
| Soil lab    | Sample processing, sequencing     | Benches, machines, contamination states         |
| Grow room   | Plants, controlled conditions     | Warm light, rows, visible decline over the acts |
| PI's office | Meetings and requests             | Status objects, awards, a closing door          |
| Corridor    | Transit, notices                  | Institutional accretion; notices change by act  |
| Break room  | Rest, colleague scenes            | Coffee, notices, the only soft light            |

## Movement and camera (approved)

- Comfortable first-person walking with keyboard, mouse, and controller.
- No head-bob, no forced camera motion, no jump scares.
- Station visits are short and purposeful: experiments require a visit;
  analysis and writing happen at the desk.
- Voss and the colleagues are found in their rooms.

## Visual language (approved)

- Minimalist and diagrammatic, inspired by _Mini Metro_.
- Clean shapes, flat colours, a restrained palette, and one pressure accent
  colour.
- Soil and laboratory processes appear as simple diagrams and states, not
  photoreal detail.
- Temporary geometry is acceptable in early builds; the final style is
  approved before final content.
- Institutional notices and room states change by act, showing accretion and
  decline: the environment carries the comedy.

## Audio (approved)

- Diegetic room ambience and restrained cues.
- A cold synth motif marks pressure and escalation.
- No voice acting; the game is text-led.
- Captions cover non-speech cues, and audio never carries required information
  alone.

## Desk board (approved)

- A diegetic desk in the shared office, with a clean overlay for claims,
  evidence, and requirements.
- The board is the main systemic surface; the 3D space provides atmosphere,
  scenes, and station actions.

## Asset plan (approved)

| Role        | Approach                                                        |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Environment | Modular geometry; reuse licensed assets or create original work |
| Props       | Reuse or create; licence-checked before integration             |
| Characters  | Stylized, silhouette-first, with a small shared animation set   |
| Interface   | Original and minimal, drawn from the approved palette           |
| Audio       | Reuse or create; licensed and credited                          |

Temporary geometry is used for the first builds. External assets enter only
after research and Leonardo's approval, under the reuse-modify-create policy
(`D-006`), and every asset is recorded in the manifest.

## Accessibility baseline (approved)

- Scale, contrast, reduced motion, keyboard-only operation, captions, and no
  colour-only information from the start.
- Detailed settings and acceptance criteria are written in Phase B.

- Exact palettes, dimensions, and asset identifiers (Phase B).
