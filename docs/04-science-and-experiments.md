# Science and Experiments

Status: **seeded; fictional mechanism unresolved**

## Scientific field and model

The protagonist is a developmental biologist studying a beating cardiac
organoid. The fictional decisive result is that, after controlled damage, the
tissue reconstructs its spatial organization and restores coordinated rhythmic
contraction.

Current science supports contracting cardiac tissue models, self-assembling
heart organoids with selected organized features, and cardiac organoid use in
some injury, disease, and drug-cardiotoxicity studies. It does **not** establish
the game's autonomous post-damage reconstruction and coordinated recovery.

The game must not describe the model as a complete miniature heart, a routine
transplantable organ, an established treatment, or demonstrated present-day
regenerative technology.

## Progressive damage ladder

1. **Original laser-injury discovery:** precise localized damage unexpectedly
   produces structural reconstruction and rhythmic recovery.
2. **Initial supplementary work:** replication, sham controls, better imaging,
   damage intensities, recovery times, tissue batches, stimulation conditions,
   and molecular markers.
3. **Mechanism work:** candidate pathways are perturbed. Some outcomes support
   the emerging account and others complicate it.
4. **Reviewer escalation:** laser damage is criticized as artificial.
   Temporary oxygen deprivation and restoration, cardiotoxic-drug exposure, or
   both are demanded.

## Experiment-family identities

- **Laser injury:** precise, visually dramatic, and controllable.
- **Oxygen deprivation and restoration:** slower, riskier, and dependent on
  environmental monitoring.
- **Cardiotoxic-drug exposure:** dose-dependent, commercially relevant, and
  vulnerable to conveniently selective interpretation.

The families should reuse organoid culture, imaging, monitoring, and analysis
systems rather than act as unrelated minigames.

## Industry relevance

A biotech company can credibly value a reproducible injury-and-recovery model
for cardiac drug-safety, disease, repair, or assay-development research. It is
interested in the method and the protagonist's culture, microscopy, image
analysis, perturbation, failure-diagnosis, and quality-control skills—not in a
miracle therapeutic heart.

## Safety and abstraction boundary

All protocols remain fictionalized and non-actionable. The game may use
recognizable conceptual stages, visual states, and quality-control logic, but
must not provide operational biological-engineering instructions, real
quantities, or executable protocols.

- Exact experiment order, dependencies, durations, failure modes, and readable
  outputs.

  them.
