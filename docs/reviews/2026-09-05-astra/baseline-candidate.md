## Alternatives considered

1. Keep costs and extend only the batch window: it does not resolve the already excessive thorough-honest lower bound (65 Supported before surcharges/window losses) or the original opening cost. It can remain an intentionally stricter alternative, but does not meet the proposed broader Supported target without reducing workload.
2. Increase recovery only: it reduces break demand but cannot remove the two independent early conflicts—four experiment stages before Week-1 ends, and unspecified monitoring offsets. It also gives recovery a wider balance change throughout the run.
3. Reduce starts, analysis or manuscript commitments instead: it saves time from the executed work or the central revision pressure. Free configuration preserves those charged actions and leaves the player choosing the same qualitative plan.
4. Expand the calendar: not selected. It changes every week/ending boundary and discards a useful fixed-semester constraint.
5. Remove all voluntary risk options: feasible if different scientific questions prove uninteresting. The candidate first gives them concrete finite authored purpose, to evaluate in the temporary loop.

These are reasoned alternatives, not an exhaustive optimization or proof that C01 is the only solution.
