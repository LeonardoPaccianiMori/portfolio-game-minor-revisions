## Source-verified high-priority findings

1. The Morrow route requires at least Developing evidence, while the campaign
   starts at Developing. Its other main requirements are the preprint and
   Camila interactions. This makes the route weakly connected to laboratory
   success.
2. The fallback removes both authored scenes in which Haoran or Gabriel can
   notice a visible record mismatch. It still keeps the complete integrity
   fork and claims to keep all Citations. `MR-CIT-08` therefore has no stated
   fallback trigger.
3. Hidden integrity problems do not by themselves close Morrow or Aldercroft.
   Inflated claims increase PI confidence, which is one Aldercroft condition.
   The current design does not define a reliable counter-cost in time or route
   readiness for hidden misconduct.
4. Primary result records provide both the observation and its interpretation.
   This weakens the stated player challenge of interpreting evidence.
5. Equipment faults, queues, booking limits, and access restrictions are
   promised as authored pressure, but the full content catalogue provides only
   one clear queue event and no general equipment-event family.
6. Much of the absurdist environmental writing is optional inspection content.
   The repeated experiment and status actions use mainly functional language.
   This creates a credible risk that the middle campaign feels administrative
   instead of consistently fun and absurdist.

## Qualifications and external-review overstatements

- Stage 3 calls the fallback “the likely shipped artifact.” The production
  plan permits the project to remain at fallback scope if the full target does
  not pass, but it does not say that this outcome is likely. Treat the phrase
  as speculation. The fallback contradiction remains valid.
- Stage 3 says integrity is “mechanically inert.” This is too broad. Visible
  evidence conflicts and a fabrication confession can affect route access,
  optional scenes, trust, and ending content. The narrower supported finding is
  that hidden integrity loss has no direct route effect and can be especially
  consequence-light in the fallback.
- “Minimum-effort Morrow route” is a supported gate analysis, not proof of
  actual player behaviour. A prototype or state-path test must confirm how the
  required manuscript and Camila flow feels in play.
- Predicted boredom, comedy failure, player optimization, and target-player
  response are hypotheses. Leonardo and Codex are the only approved play
  evaluators, so these points require private slice testing rather than being
  treated as measured facts.
